How to get in

The Dancefloor is for members of the route. No guest list, no plus ones, no line.

  1. 1

    Get on the route

    Sign up for the monthly drop. That's the only ticket there is.

  2. 2

    Check your envelope

    Your key to the floor arrives with your first drop.

  3. 3

    Step in

    Say hi, pick a room, post a tune.

Doors aren't open yet. They open with Drop 001.

House rules

Keep it PLUR

  1. Peace. Disagree about music all you like. Be kind about people.
  2. Love. Hype each other up. Somebody's first mix deserves a listen.
  3. Unity. Every genre, every scene, every age of raver is welcome here.
  4. Respect. Credit artists, ask before reposting, and no means no.
  5. Music first. No spam, no sourcing, no ticket sales, no scams.
