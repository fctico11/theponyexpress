Backstory

How a finger puppet got a mail route

  1. 01

    A puppet walks into a rave

    Marty is a zebra finger puppet. He started coming along to raves on one finger, held up above the crowd. No big plan. He just liked the music, and the view from up there.

  2. 02

    People kept coming over

    Strangers pointed, laughed and came over to say hi. Nobody's in a bad mood around a zebra in sunglasses. He turned out to be the easiest way to meet people on a dancefloor, and a pretty good reminder of what PLUR is meant to feel like.

  3. 03

    Then he got a job

    The music always stops eventually, but Marty wasn't ready to let that go. He wanted a way to keep those connections going after the lights came up, so now he carries the mail for The Zebra Express. He still goes out. If you see stripes above the crowd, that's him.
