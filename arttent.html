What we're hanging

    Open to everyone, members or not. One to five pieces per drop. No AI-generated art.

    How it works

    From your wall to every mailbox

    1. 1

      Make something

      Read the brief and make it yours. New work or something you've been sitting on, as long as it comes from the floor.

    2. 2

      Send it in

      Upload it below. Tell us your name and where people can find you. Submissions stay in the running for upcoming drops.

    3. 3

      Check your inbox

      When your piece is picked for a drop, we'll email you before it goes out. Then it lands in every member's mailbox.

    Featured artists get credit in print, a spot on the wall below and a free copy of the drop.

    The drop box

    Submit your art

    Images up to 20 MB each, up to five at a time. JPG, PNG, WEBP or PDF. Scans and photos of physical pieces are welcome.

    Before you send

    House rules

    1. Your own work. Made by you, or by you and friends who are happy to be credited.
    2. No AI. We don't accept AI-generated art. This one's for human hands only.
    3. Keep it PLUR. Nothing hateful, nothing cruel, nothing that would get you kicked out of a party.
    4. Respect the artists. No other people's logos, artwork or characters passed off as your own.
    5. Fit the page. We might crop or resize to fit the envelope. We won't change the work itself.
    6. No guarantees. We can't feature everything. If it isn't picked, keep sending new work. Every drop is a new theme.
