The basics

What is The Zebra Express?

A snail-mail club for ravers. Once a month we pick one sound, one room or one era and pack an envelope with things we love about it. Then Marty sends it to your door.

What's in the envelope?

That's the fun part: it changes every month, and we keep it a surprise until it lands. Every GA envelope has the zine, a flyer print and stickers, letters from the floor and a quiz. VIP adds a kandi single, a trinket and a 2x2 perler. No spoilers beyond that.

What's the difference between GA and VIP?

GA is all the flat stuff: the zine, a flyer print, stickers, letters and the quiz, in a flat envelope. VIP is everything in GA plus a hand-strung kandi single, a trinket and a 2x2 perler every month, so it travels in a padded mailer. Compare the tickets.

How do I sign up?

Get on the route with your email and we'll tell you the moment Drop 001 is ready to send.

Price and billing

How much does it cost?

GA is $6 a month, or $60 for the year paid up front. VIP is $10 a month, or $100 for the year. Paying for the year gets you two months free either way, and shipping within the US is included.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, no questions asked. Cancel before the 1st and you won't be charged for the next drop. If you paid for the year, your envelopes keep coming until your year is up.

Can I switch between GA and VIP?

Yes. Switch before the 1st and the change starts with that month's drop. If you paid for the year, we'll work out the difference with you.

When am I charged?

Monthly members are charged when they sign up, then once a month before each drop goes out. Yearly members pay once, up front.

Can I send it as a gift?

Not yet, but it's on the list. Follow @thezebraexpress to hear when it's ready.

Shipping

Where do you ship?

The United States only, for now. We'd love to go further, and we'll shout about it when we can.

When does my envelope go out?

Every envelope leaves on the 1st of the month. Join before then to get that month's drop. Join after, and your first one goes out on the 1st of the next month.

How long does it take to arrive?

It's real mail, so it moves at mail speed: usually within a week of the 1st, depending on where you live. GA comes as a flat envelope and VIP as a padded mailer, but both leave on the same day.

I moved. How do I change my address?

Update it before the 1st and that month's drop goes to your new place.

My envelope never showed up.

Mail happens. If it hasn't arrived by the 15th, DM us and we'll sort it out.

Shop and returns

Do shop orders ship to the same places?

Yes: shop orders ship within the US only, just like the monthly mail.

Can I return something from the shop?

Yes. If it's not right, send it back unworn and in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery and we'll refund you. DM us to start a return.

It arrived broken.

Kandi takes a beating sometimes. Send us a photo within 30 days and we'll replace it or refund you, your pick.
